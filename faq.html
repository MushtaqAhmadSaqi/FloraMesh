Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our turnkey planting visits, IoT soil sensors, gift deliveries, and native tree planting initiatives.

How does the turnkey planting service work in Islamabad and Rawalpindi?
When you order a turnkey balcony, terrace, or indoor package online, you choose your space size and schedule a convenient arrival window. Our vetted field crew arrives with nursery-acclimated plants, breathable Multani or Taxila pots, and organic Seedistry potting substrate, completes the setup in 2–4 hours, and leaves your balcony spotless.
Which neighborhoods do your gardening crews cover?
Our vans serve all sectors of Islamabad (E, F, G, H, I, Bani Gala, Park View City) and Rawalpindi (Bahria Town Phases 1–8, DHA Phases 1–3, Chaklala Scheme 3, Westridge, Satellite Town, and Cantt). For other cities in Pakistan, we ship our dry goods, planters, and IoT sensors via nationwide courier.
How does the FloraMesh Smart Soil Sensor connect to my phone?
Each FloraMesh sensor probe measures root-zone moisture percentage, soil pH balance, and ambient temperature. The device pairs via Bluetooth Low Energy during initial setup and transmits telemetry over your home Wi-Fi or cellular LoRa gateway, sending automated alerts before plants show underwatering distress.
Can the sensor probes remain outdoors during monsoon rains?
Yes. The sensor probe head is IP65 weather-resistant and sealed with silicon gaskets to withstand intense Potohar monsoon downpours and overhead garden hose watering. Avoid completely submerging the top housing in standing puddle water.
Do gift hampers include prices on the packing slip?
Never. When you add artisan gift wrap or enter a custom recipient note, we omit all PKR prices from the physical delivery slip. The package arrives neatly boxed with your personalized message handwritten on a botanical card.
What are the delivery fees and free shipping thresholds?
Delivery across Islamabad and Rawalpindi is completely free on all orders of Rs 10,000 and above. For orders under Rs 10,000, our standard van delivery fee is Rs 450. Digital subscriptions and Margalla CSR tree donation tokens are always 100% exempt from delivery fees.
What happens if a ceramic planter arrives chipped or a plant struggles?
Any planter damaged in courier transit is replaced promptly upon sending a photo within 48 hours of delivery. In addition, all turnkey planted packages include a 14-day establishment support window where our horticultural team advises on acclimation, lighting, and watering adjustments.
Why do you use Taxila terracotta instead of plastic gamlas?
During June temperatures reaching 45°C on concrete balconies, plastic pots act like ovens, overheating root systems. Fired Taxila terracotta is microporous, naturally breathing and dissipating thermal heat through evaporative cooling, which preserves beneficial soil microbes.
How are Margalla Native Tree CSR Tokens verified?
Every CSR token funds a native Potohar sapling (such as Amaltas, Kachnar, or Chir Pine) planted during seasonal student volunteer drives across Islamabad's green belts. Contributors receive a laser-engraved wooden token and a digital record noting the planting site GPS coordinates and 60-day survival monitoring check.
