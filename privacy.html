Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026. This policy outlines our handling of delivery details, customer contacts, and IoT environmental telemetry.

1. Information We Collect

When you book an on-site balcony planting appointment, place an order for ceramics or IoT hardware, or subscribe to our seasonal care alerts, we collect contact information including your full name, phone number, email address, and delivery coordinates across Islamabad and Rawalpindi. If you deploy FloraMesh Smart Soil Sensors, our telemetry backend receives soil moisture percentages, pH values, and ambient temperature readings solely for plant health diagnostics.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information exclusively to schedule field gardening visits, coordinate courier dispatches, compute automated watering recommendations, and verify geo-tagged Margalla CSR tree donations. We never sell, lease, or monetize your personal details with third-party advertising brokers.

3. Local Browser Storage & Cookies

FloraMesh uses local browser storage (localStorage) to maintain your shopping cart, preferred color theme (Heritage Cream / Midnight Grove), and saved wishlists across browser sessions without requiring invasive third-party tracking cookies.

4. Contacting Our Data Custodian

If you wish to inspect, update, or request deletion of your contact information or sensor telemetry logs, please reach out to our team at hello@floramesh.pk.
