Our Botanical Heritage & Mission

“Aap ka khayal, hamara poda, sarsabz jahan.”

FloraMesh is a digital-first smart landscaping house founded in Islamabad. We remove the guesswork from urban greening by pairing precision IoT soil telemetry with authentic Pakistani pottery and climate-resilient horticulture calibrated for the Potohar plateau.

Potohar Ecology First

Islamabad and Rawalpindi experience 45°C pre-monsoon heat followed by monsoon downpours and winter frosts. We cultivate only species proven to thrive through all five regional micro-seasons.

Connected Telemetry

Overwatering causes 80% of urban plant mortality. Our wireless capacitive sensors monitor root-zone moisture, soil pH, and temperature directly at soil level, taking human intuition into empirical science.

Living Pakistani Craft

We work directly with craft kilns in Taxila and Multan to produce breathable terracotta and Kashigari glazed planters that insulate roots from scorching balcony concrete.

Horticultural Calendar

Tuned to the 5 Seasons of Islamabad

Dec – Jan

Kohar (Frost Chills)

Deep root mulching, reduced watering, and cold-frame frost cloths protect tender bougainvillea.

Feb – Mar

Bahar (Spring Bloom)

Vigorous repotting season with Seedistry living organic compost and seasonal heirloom seed propagation.

Apr – Jun

Garmi (Intense Heat)

Shade cloth positioning and automated twice-daily telemetry alerts to prevent root dehydration.

Jul – Aug

Barsat (Monsoon)

Aerated drainage trays prevent root rot while monsoon rainwater fuels lush native vegetative growth.

Sep – Nov

Khazan (Golden Autumn)

Nitrogen replenishment and winter bulb bedding across Islamabad's terrace gardens.
