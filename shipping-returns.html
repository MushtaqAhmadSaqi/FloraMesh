2. Transit Damage Replacement (48-Hour Guarantee)

Artisan terracotta and glazed ceramic pottery are delicate. Although packed in shock-absorbing honeycomb wrap, courier accidents occasionally happen.

How to claim replacement: 1. Take 2 clear photos showing the broken/chipped section and the shipping label.
2. Email them to hello@floramesh.pk within 48 hours of delivery.
3. Our team will dispatch an identical replacement planter at zero additional charge.

3. Plant Establishment & Returns

14-Day Nursery Check-In Window: Plants adjust to new balconies and lighting. If any plant in a turnkey package shows signs of transplant stress within 14 days, send a photo to our horticultural team for custom watering, shade, and nutrition advice.
